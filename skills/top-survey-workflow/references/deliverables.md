# Deliverables

## Minimum Artifact Set

- survey viability brief
- differentiated-against-prior-surveys note
- paper corpus spreadsheet or markdown table
- final taxonomy outline
- section-by-section synthesis notes
- figure plan
- comparison table plan
- internal review memo
- submission checklist

## Quality Gates

### Gate 1: Scope

- Topic is not too broad for one review
- Reader and venue are explicit
- Recent related surveys are identified

### Gate 2: Taxonomy

- Sections are mutually intelligible and reasonably non-overlapping
- Representative papers fit the structure
- The taxonomy itself feels publishable

### Gate 3: Critique

- The paper does not read like an annotated bibliography
- At least one major tension or contradiction is surfaced in each main section
- Future directions are specific, not generic

### Gate 4: Final Packaging

- Abstract makes a strong contribution claim without overselling
- Figures and tables can stand on their own
- Formatting and references are venue-ready
