# Awesome AI Research Writing Prompt Map

Adapted from `Leey21/awesome-ai-research-writing`.

## Prompt Families

- `中转英`: Chinese draft to clean English LaTeX academic prose.
- `英转中`: English LaTeX to readable Chinese explanation.
- `中转中`: Chinese academic rewriting for Word-style manuscripts.
- `缩写`: shorten text without losing meaning.
- `扩写`: expand underdeveloped text while preserving the claim.
- `表达润色`: polish English or Chinese paper prose.
- `逻辑检查`: inspect argument flow and hidden leaps.
- `去 AI 味`: remove mechanical AI style and excessive transitions.
- `论文架构图`: design architecture/pipeline figure prompts.
- `实验绘图推荐`: choose chart types and figure layouts.
- `生成图的标题` / `生成表的标题`: caption drafting.
- `实验分析`: interpret results with claim boundaries.
- `Reviewer 视角`: adversarial full-paper review.
- `模型选择`: choose writing/reasoning model for the task.

## Codex Use

Prefer local specialized skills when they are more precise:

- `research-paper-writing` for paper section flow.
- `nature-polishing` for final scientific English.
- `figures4papers` or `nature-figure` for figures.
- `pre-submission-reviewer` or `top-ai-paper-submission` for full-paper risk.
