## Scoring

Score each idea from 1 to 5:

- Predictive plausibility
- Point-in-time feasibility
- Implementation speed
- Robustness to regime shift
- Portfolio usefulness after costs

## Minimum Experiment Standard

Every idea needs:

- clear universe and period
- point-in-time feature contract
- benchmark comparison
- cost and turnover awareness
- out-of-sample or walk-forward split
- kill criteria before looking at results
