# LaTeX and Reviewer Checklist

## LaTeX and references

- [ ] document compiles with no undefined citations/references
- [ ] labels are unique and targets correct
- [ ] equations are numbered/referenced only when useful
- [ ] equation punctuation integrates with sentences
- [ ] bibliography entries are complete and cited
- [ ] venue style, anonymity, page limit, and supplementary rules are satisfied
- [ ] capitalization, nonbreaking spaces, and abbreviations are consistent

## Rendered PDF

- [ ] all pages inspected visually
- [ ] figures/tables readable at final scale
- [ ] no clipping, overflow, accidental whitespace, or float misplacement
- [ ] embedded figure terms and symbols are current
- [ ] line breaks do not create ambiguity in equations or captions
- [ ] color remains interpretable under likely print/accessibility conditions

## Reviewer attack

- [ ] gap is not a straw man
- [ ] novelty is an exact distinction, not an adjective
- [ ] correlations are not presented as causes
- [ ] baselines and compute/data budgets are fair
- [ ] ablations isolate the claimed factors
- [ ] assumptions and failure cases are visible
- [ ] statistical reporting supports `significant` or equivalent language
- [ ] reproducibility details are sufficient
- [ ] headline claims survive removal of promotional wording
- [ ] limitations bound the takeaway honestly

## Final triage

Fix in order:

1. incorrect science, broken compilation, stale/unreadable assets;
2. missing logic, overclaim, inconsistency, unfair evidence;
3. ambiguity, redundancy, terminology drift;
4. optional rhythm and polish.
