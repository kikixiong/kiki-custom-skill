# Evidence Policy

## Rule classes

- **Explicit:** directly requested or stated by the user.
- **Strongly Inferred:** repeatedly selected across independent revisions.
- **Project-Specific:** scientifically or narratively tied to one paper.
- **Provisional:** plausible guidance with limited user-specific evidence.

## Promotion policy

Promote a rule to high-confidence user preference only when:

- the user states it explicitly; or
- at least two meaningful, independent decisions support it and no later
  decision contradicts it.

Do not infer a stable preference from punctuation, a one-off constraint, or a
change forced by project semantics.

## Conflict policy

When sources conflict:

1. direct user feedback beats derived summaries;
2. verified version decisions beat generic templates;
3. repeated evidence beats one-off rules;
4. project-neutral rules beat project-specific defaults for new papers;
5. scientific accuracy beats style preference.
