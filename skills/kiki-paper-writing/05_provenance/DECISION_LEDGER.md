## How to use this ledger

- Use D01–D17 for Title, Abstract, and Introduction version choices.
- Use D18–D27 for Method structure, notation, and claim decisions.
- Use D12–D14 and D28 for evidence figures and captions.
- Treat the generalized knowledge column as the rule; treat local candidate
  wording as evidence, not a universal template.
